Meet memly — Idea & Memory Sanctuary, an open-source space designed to help you capture, preserve, revisit, and rediscover your thoughts and memories.

We often collect ideas, notes, reflections, and little moments throughout our lives. But many of them get buried in folders, forgotten apps, or scattered documents.

I wanted to build something different: a personal sanctuary where memories aren't just stored — they can be revisited and explored.
✨ Key Features
- 📝 Memory Capture: Save thoughts, ideas, reflections, and personal moments.
- 🧠 AI Memory Synthesis (Echoes): Explore connections and recurring themes in your saved memories.
- 🪐 Memory Constellation: Discover relationships between memories through a visual experience.
- 🕰️ Timeline: Revisit your memories over time.
- 🎙️ Voice Capture: Capture thoughts using voice.
- 💬 AI Reflections: Ask questions and explore your archive using AI.
- 📦 Export Options: Preserve your archive in JSON, Markdown, and CSV formats.
